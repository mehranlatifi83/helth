Privacy Policy

App: Roozara  |  Last updated: June 2026

Overview

Roozara is a personal day companion whose current tools focus on sleep and wellness. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Data We Collect

Roozara does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data you enter (sleep schedule, meal times, preferences) is stored exclusively on your device using Android SharedPreferences and is never shared with third parties.

Permissions Explained

  • Do Not Disturb access — used to silence your phone during sleep mode. We do not read your notification content.
  • VPN (network blocking) — used to block internet access during sleep mode. No traffic is logged, inspected, or transmitted anywhere.
  • Accessibility (sleep guard) — optional. While sleep mode is running, it lets Roozara see which app came to the foreground so it can bring the sleep screen back and close the notification panel. Roozara does not read the content of your screen, does not see what you type, and does not interfere with TalkBack or any other screen reader. It does nothing at all outside your sleep hours, and nothing it observes is stored or transmitted. Calls are always left alone.
  • Display over other apps — used to show the sleep lock screen over any foreground app when bedtime arrives.
  • Schedule exact alarms — used to fire your sleep and wake-up schedule at the correct time.
  • Full-screen intent — used to show the wake-up screen when your alarm fires.
  • Receive Boot Completed — used to restore your schedule after the device restarts.
  • Post notifications — used to show sleep reminders and wake-up notifications.

Third-Party Services

Roozara does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Changes

If this policy changes in a future version, the updated date at the top of this document will reflect the change.
